What the numbers say

Where. 2 Critical + 9 High stations, all on the periphery and the airport/NH corridors: Int. Airport (Devanahalli) 84, Yelahanka 80, Electronic City 67, Chikkajala 66, Whitefield 64, Kengeri 60, Hennur, Mico Layout, City Market, Indiranagar, Upparpet.

Independent check: BTP's own 2023 top-5 by deaths = Yelahanka, Devanahalli, Kengeri, Whitefield, K R Puram — all in our top 12.

Why. Peripheral stations kill because crashes are lethal, not frequent: Int. Airport 1 in 4 crashes fatal vs 1 in 6 city-wide; 54–57% of fatal crashes at Yelahanka / Int. Airport / Electronic City are on NH or arterials. Whitefield: 54% of fatal crashes are hit-and-run. Core stations (Cubbon Park, Upparpet) score on trend: deaths rising 11–18%/yr from a low base.

When. 43% of deaths 18:00–02:00; Sat+Sun+Mon = 47%. Who. 9 in 10 dead are motorcyclists or pedestrians; correct helmet use 34%; 31% of vehicles over the limit.

What first, city-wide. Helmet compliance ≈ 279 lives and speed management ≈ 273 lives over 2026–28 if applied everywhere; pedestrian infrastructure 156; night enforcement 147; black-spot engineering 92 (concentrated in 12 stations).

Limits we state up front — and iteration 2

  • Unit = police jurisdiction, not road segment (only 30% of FIRs have coordinates)
  • 2024–25 deaths estimated from fatal-crash counts (× 1.036), flagged in the app
  • Forecast = straight line on 6 annual points — a baseline for arithmetic
  • Effect sizes = literature averages; costs = order-of-magnitude placeholders
  • Who/when shares are city-wide; only pedestrian / hit-and-run / highway / junction shares are station-specific
  • No exposure denominator per station (traffic, road-km) → burden-weighted score

Dropped on purpose for 3 hours: Bayesian spatio-temporal model, SHAP/LIME, graph recommender, SUMO simulation, KMeans, live traffic.
